How to Perform Each Sign

Follow the same movement you used while recording the training data. Keep your upper body and hands visible in the camera.

1. Hi

Raise one hand and wave it toward the camera.

Tip: Make a clear, repeated waving movement.

2. How are you?

Turn your hand upside down and wave it horizontally.

Tip: Keep the hand orientation and horizontal movement consistent.

3. I am fine

Raise your hand and make a clear thumbs-up gesture.

Tip: Hold the thumbs-up steadily so the camera can capture it.

4. Thankyou

Perform the hand-to-forehead gesture you used when collecting this sign's training examples.

Tip: Repeat the same movement and hand position used during training.

5. Great

Raise your hand to your forehead and perform a salute.

Tip: Make the salute clearly and hold it briefly.

These are the custom gestures used to train this SIGNSENSE model; they are not presented as standardized sign-language definitions.
